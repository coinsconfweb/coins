COINS 2019 — 1st Edition

May 5–7, 2019 · Crete, Greece · In-person

📁 Archived Record

Content preserved from the original coinsconf.com/2019 site ahead of its retirement. The original site (while still online) can be found at https://coinsconf.com/2019.

IoT | Artificial Intelligence | Machine Learning | Big Data | Cloud | Sensor | Embedded Systems | Computer Architecture | EDA

The inaugural IEEE International Conference on Omni-Layer Intelligent Systems was held at the Aldemar Knossos Royal & Royal Villas in Crete, launching the conference's focus on omni-layer intelligent systems spanning IoT, AI, embedded systems, and computer architecture, with vertical application tracks in Health, Smart City, Automotive, Industry 4.0, and Agriculture.
