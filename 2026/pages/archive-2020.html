COINS 2020 — 2nd Edition

August 31 – September 2, 2020 · Virtual

📁 Archived Record

The original 2020.coinsconf.com site was already unreachable (HTTP 500) at archiving time; content was reconstructed from IEEE ComSoc's call-for-papers mirror, the IEEE Xplore proceedings listing, and public web sources. The original site (while still online) can be found at https://2020.coinsconf.com.

International Conference on Omni-layer INtelligent Systems | Internet of Things, Big Data, Artificial Intelligence Conference

The 2nd IEEE International Conference on Omni-Layer Intelligent Systems was originally planned as an in-person event in Barcelona, Spain, but was postponed and converted to a fully virtual conference due to the COVID-19 pandemic. The program combined prerecorded presentations with live sessions, covering novel IoT technologies from connected devices through edge/fog and cloud to omni-layer artificial intelligence and machine learning.

Program

Held as a virtual conference with plenary keynote speakers, invited and regular paper sessions, panels, special hot-topic sessions, and workshops; authors gave prerecorded presentations (15–25 minutes) followed by live discussion sessions via Webex.

📄 Proceedings

Accepted papers were published in IEEE Xplore as part of the COINS 2020 proceedings.
