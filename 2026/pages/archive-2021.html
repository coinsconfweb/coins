COINS 2021 — 3rd Edition

August 23–26, 2021 · Virtual

📁 Archived Record

Limited record: the coinsconf.com/2021 pages mostly carry leftover COINS 2020 content. Dates and keynotes above come from the official 2021 general program (Program-General-S7-2021.pdf); no reliable 2021-specific committee or sponsor details could be found. The original site (while still online) can be found at https://coinsconf.com/2021.

The 3rd IEEE International Conference on Omni-Layer Intelligent Systems was held as a fully virtual event, with free registration for attendees.
