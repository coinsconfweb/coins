COINS 2022 — 4th Edition

August 1–3, 2022 · Barcelona, Spain · Hybrid (in-person & virtual)

📁 Archived Record

Content preserved from the original coinsconf.com/2022 site ahead of its retirement. The original site (while still online) can be found at https://coinsconf.com/2022.

Digital Transformation — from AI and IoT to Cloud, Blockchain, Cybersecurity, and Robotics

The 4th IEEE International Conference on Omni-Layer Intelligent Systems was hosted at the Hotel Novotel Barcelona City, bringing together researchers, engineers, and industry leaders across IoT, Big Data, AI, Cloud Computing, Blockchain, Cybersecurity, and Robotics with a multi-disciplinary program of technical papers, workshops, tutorials, special sessions, and a PhD Forum.

Topics

Program

The program included a keynote lecture, technical paper sessions with on-site and virtual presentations, workshops, tutorials, special sessions, and a PhD Forum.

📄 Proceedings

Accepted papers (max. 6 pages, double-column, 10pt, with page charges beyond 6 up to an 8-page limit) were published in IEEE proceedings and distributed via IEEE Xplore, copyright held by IEEE.
