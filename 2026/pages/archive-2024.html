COINS 2024 — 6th Edition

July 29–31, 2024 · London, UK · Hybrid (in-person & virtual)

📁 Archived Record

Content preserved from the original coinsconf.com/2024 site ahead of its retirement. The original site (while still online) can be found at https://coinsconf.com/2024.

Digital Transformation — from AI and IoT to Cloud, Blockchain, Cybersecurity, and Robotics

The 6th IEEE International Conference on Omni-Layer Intelligent Systems was hosted at King's College London, bringing together researchers, engineers, and industry leaders across IoT, Big Data, AI, Cloud Computing, Blockchain, Cybersecurity, and Robotics with a multi-disciplinary program of technical papers, panels, workshops, and tutorials.

Venue

King's College London — Strand Campus

Strand, WC2R 2LS
London, United Kingdom

Keynote Speakers

🎤
Nikil Dutt
University of California, Irvine, USA
Omni-Layer Intelligence Requires Cross-Layer Self-Awareness
Argued that future systems need computational self-awareness through introspection, combined with adaptive mechanisms operating across multiple layers of the compute stack.
🎤
Julie A. McCann
Imperial College London, UK
Finding Rubies in the Dust
Addressed reliability challenges in wireless sensor networks and IoT, and how to improve robustness in real-world networked deployments.
🎤
Partha Maji
Tenstorrent Inc.
Speeding Up Generative AI
Examined hardware accelerator architectures and optimization strategies for deploying large language models on emerging spatial computing platforms.

Program

The program included technical paper sessions, special sessions, workshops, a PhD Forum, and a Diversity & Inclusion track alongside the three keynote lectures.
