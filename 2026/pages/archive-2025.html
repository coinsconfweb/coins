COINS 2025 — 7th Edition

August 4–6, 2025 · Madison, Wisconsin, USA · Hybrid (in-person & virtual)

📁 Archived Record

Content preserved from the original coinsconf.com/2025 site ahead of its retirement. The original site (while still online) can be found at https://coinsconf.com/2025.

International Conference on Omni-layer Intelligent Systems

The 7th IEEE International Conference on Omni-Layer Intelligent Systems was hosted at the Pyle Center, University of Wisconsin–Madison, bringing together experts in Digital Transformation — from AI and IoT to Cloud, Blockchain, Cybersecurity, and Robotics — with a multi-disciplinary program of technical research papers, panels, workshops, and tutorials on the latest developments across the IoT & AI ecosystem.

Keynote Speakers

🎤
Diana Marculescu
Department Chair and Cockrell Family Chair for Engineering Leadership, University of Texas at Austin
Energy Efficient Generative AI from Cloud to Edge
Addressed the gap between ML model accuracy and actual energy efficiency, latency, and battery lifetime, discussing platform-specific power models and hardware-aware design for efficient transformer and diffusion models across modalities.
🎤
Mondira (Mandy) Deb Pant
TA-Chief of Staff, Lead Technologist, Intel Design Engineering Group
Moore's Law in the AI era
Explored Moore's Law's impact on technological breakthroughs and its role in today's AI landscape, addressing workforce development challenges and environmental impacts affecting future AI progress.
🎤
Vivek Prasad
Design Ecosystem Enablement, Natcast (operator of the National Semiconductor Technology Center)
Chips Act & Systems of Support for US Semiconductor Ecosystem
Detailed NSTC's implementation of the US CHIPS and Science Act, focusing on design enablement, silicon aggregation, and wraparound support for semiconductor research and startups.
🎤
Randall Berry
Chair and John A. Dever Professor, Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering, Northwestern University
Meeting the Spectrum Needs of Next Generation Wireless Systems
Analyzed spectrum allocation trade-offs for the 3 GHz and 7-8 GHz bands using game-theoretic models to evaluate the impact on commercial wireless services.
