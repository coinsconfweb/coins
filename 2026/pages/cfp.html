Call for Papers

Original research on intelligent systems spanning hardware, AI, networks, and applications. Deadline: April 29, 2026.

📅 Submission Deadline: April 29, 2026

Complete manuscripts submitted via EasyChair. Double-blind review policy — anonymize your manuscript.

Submission Format

  • ✦ Up to 6 pages (IEEE two-column, 10pt font)
  • ✦ Optional 2 extra pages at $150/page
  • ✦ PDF format only, with savable text
  • ✦ Double-blind — no author names/affiliations
  • ✦ Use IEEE Proceedings Template

Review Process

  • ✦ Blind peer review by multiple reviewers
  • ✦ Criteria: Relevance, Originality, Technical Quality
  • ✦ Full papers: 20+5 min oral presentation
  • ✦ Short papers: 13+2 min oral presentation
  • ✦ Published in IEEE Xplore
