Student Travel Grants

COINS Executive Committee offers registration grants for highly skilled students with an accepted paper. Grants are awarded on a competitive basis. Applications from students in the Middle East and Asia are especially encouraged.

Apply: coins@coinsconf.com
🌍

RAS CARES

IEEE COINS 2026 adheres to the IEEE RAS CARES initiative — a commitment to a safe, respectful, and inclusive environment. CARES provides confidential support for attendees who experience or witness harassment or discrimination.

Learn about RAS CARES →
🤝

Diversity, Equity & Inclusion

IEEE COINS 2026 welcomes participants from all backgrounds, career stages, geographies, and institutions. Special consideration is given to underrepresented communities in STEM through grant programs, outreach talks, and mentoring sessions.

🚀

Workforce Development

COINS organizes career development sessions, job fairs, and networking events connecting PhD students and early-career researchers with industry leaders and academic hiring managers.

Code of Conduct

All participants are expected to adhere to the IEEE Code of Conduct. Harassment or disrespectful behaviour will not be tolerated. Report violations to conference organizers or via the RAS CARES channel.
