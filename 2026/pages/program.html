🎙 Panel 2 — Diversity & Inclusion
Room A (Rooms B–C reserved for audience) · 100 min
🎙 Moderator: Prof. Sara Vinco — Politecnico di Torino
Topic 1 — Navigating an Engineering Career as a Woman: Insights and Lessons Learned
This panel will provide a platform for leading women in technology, engineering, and research to share their experiences, perspectives, and lessons learned across academia, industry, and the innovation ecosystem. Panelists will reflect on their successes and challenges in navigating the tech industry and research environment, offering insights into the role of practical, technical, entrepreneurial, and relational skills in building sustainable careers. The panel will also create an open dialogue on visibility, mentorship, career development, leadership, and relationship building, while emphasizing the central role that women engineers and researchers play in shaping the future of intelligent technologies.
• Prof. Cecilia Metra — University of Bologna, Italy• Maria Cristina Borelli — Silicon Catalyst, Italy• Dr. Annachiara Ruospo — Politecnico di Torino, Italy• Dr. Valeria Tomaselli — STMicroelectronics, Italy
Topic 2 — Responsible AI by Design: Perspectives from Research and Industry
As artificial intelligence becomes increasingly embedded in everyday technologies, the design of intelligent systems must account not only for performance, but also for trust, accessibility, fairness, robustness, and real-world impact. This panel will explore how choices made throughout the AI lifecycle influence who benefits from AI and how these systems behave in practice. The panel will highlight practical challenges, lessons learned, and concrete actions for building intelligent technologies that serve society responsibly.
• Prof. Leila Ismail — UAE University, UAE• Prof. Michela Milano — University of Bologna, Italy• Dr. Federica Zonzini — University of Bologna, Italy • Maria Cristina Borelli — Silicon Catalyst, Italy
