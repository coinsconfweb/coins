Call for Sponsorship

Partner with IEEE COINS 2026 and reach an international community of researchers, engineers, and industry leaders in intelligent systems.

IEEE COINS partners with a limited number of organizations each year to help support the conference program, student travel grants, and outreach initiatives. Sponsors gain direct visibility with an international audience of researchers, engineers, and industry leaders working across the omni-layer AI stack — from silicon to cloud.

✦ Direct visibility with 500+ attendees from 50+ countries, spanning academia and industry.
✦ Logo placement on the conference website, printed program, and on-site signage.
✦ Opportunities to host recruiting tables, product demos, and industry talks.
✦ Support student travel grants and outreach programs that grow the community.
