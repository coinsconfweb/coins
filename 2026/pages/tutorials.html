Tutorials

Deep-dive technical tutorials on emerging and foundational topics in intelligent systems. Half-day and full-day sessions by leading experts.

Tutorial · Half-day

From TinyML to EdgeAI — Tutorial and Practical Examples

DP
Danilo Pau — STMicroelectronics, Italy

This tutorial covers the full spectrum from TinyML to Edge AI, with hands-on practical examples on real hardware and software frameworks. Participants will learn model optimization techniques, deployment workflows on microcontrollers and edge devices, and gain first-hand experience with industry-grade tools and pipelines.

Tutorial · Half-day

RISC-V Architectures and Ecosystem

University of Bologna — ETH Zurich Team — University of Bologna / ETH Zurich

This tutorial introduces the RISC-V open instruction set architecture and its growing ecosystem of processors, extensions, and tools. Topics include the RISC-V ISA, custom extensions for AI acceleration, open-source toolchains, and the PULP platform developed at the University of Bologna and ETH Zurich.
