Bologna is the capital of the Emilia-Romagna region in northern Italy, famous for its mediaeval towers and porticoes (UNESCO World Heritage), vibrant food scene (birthplace of ragu and mortadella), and its university — one of the world's oldest. Excellent rail connections from Milan, Florence, and Rome. September brings warm, pleasant weather ideal for exploring the city between sessions.

🚌 Getting There

The venue is in the city centre with limited parking. Take TPER bus lines from Bologna Centrale train station. Nearest parking: Parcheggio Piazza VIII Agosto or Parcheggio Stazione Bologna Centrale.
