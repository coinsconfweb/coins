VISA Information

Bologna, Italy is in the Schengen Area. Many nationalities require a visa for short stays.

⚠️ Apply Early

VISA processing times vary by country. We strongly recommend applying at least 6–8 weeks before travel. Conference organizers can provide an official invitation letter.

Do I Need a VISA?

Italy is a member of the Schengen Area. EU/EEA/Swiss citizens do not need a VISA. Many other nationalities (US, UK, Canada, Australia, Japan, South Korea…) can enter for short stays without a VISA. Citizens of many other countries require a Schengen Short-Stay (C) VISA. Check your country's requirements at the Italian Ministry of Foreign Affairs VISA portal.

Invitation Letter

Registered authors and attendees requiring an official invitation letter should contact coins@coinsconf.com after completing registration. Please include: full name (as on passport), passport number and expiry, institution/affiliation, country of residence, paper title (if applicable), and proof of registration.
